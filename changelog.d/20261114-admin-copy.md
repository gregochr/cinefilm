### Changed — Admin buttons say what they do

Admin buttons now say what they do to what: "Find the film…", "Sort out editions…", "Scan library now", "Refresh all ratings", "Import from Plex again", "Try connecting to Plex" and "Stop this stream". A plain line under each button says what happens, so it works on a phone too, and the line under Remembered networks now describes what Forget really does. "Scan library now" is the only amber button on the Admin pages; a dialog's own confirm button keeps its amber.
