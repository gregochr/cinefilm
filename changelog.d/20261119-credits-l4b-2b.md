### Added — credits classifier: the encoder benchmark (L4b-2b)

`CreditsEncoderBenchMain` times an image encoder on 520 synthetic frames (the product's JPEG round trip, then the encoder, in batches of 16) and writes `bench-<stamp>/bench.json` with the open time, CPU and wall time per frame and per film against the two-minute limit, peak RSS (from `/proc/self/status`, null on the Mac) and the heap limit; the planner runs it on each candidate encoder at L4a. The synthetic frames are made outside the timed part, a batch at a time. Nothing in the server calls it. Its tests use a pure-Java stub encoder and a fake clock, so only one end-to-end test needs ONNX Runtime and runs on Linux in CI.
