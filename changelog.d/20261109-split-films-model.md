### Added — Groundwork for films split across several files

Nothing changes on screen yet. This lays the foundations for films that come as "pt1" and "pt2" files, and for
editions that group themselves: the database now knows a file's part number, which films you have settled by hand and
who grouped each edition; an edition can total the length of several parts; and the file-name reader (title, year,
`{edition-…}` tag, part suffix, TMDB/IMDb ids) exists and is tested, ready for the scanner to use in the next step.
