### Added — Full English subtitles in the player

When a film has a full English subtitle file beside it, the Subtitles menu now offers it as "English (full)", right after Off, and the C key cycles through it. It is on by default for a film that needs full subtitles when nothing has been chosen and the selected audio is not labelled English. Choosing anything, Off included, is remembered and kept. A film's subtitle setting now stays empty until someone chooses, so "never chosen" can be told from "switched Off".
