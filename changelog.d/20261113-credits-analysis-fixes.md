### Changed — credits

The credits analysis now reads the credits text real films have. A mostly dark frame measures its text against its own brightest pixels (never below half or above the whole window's peak), so thin or dim lettering counts; frames with 70% dark pixels (dense multi-column credits) can be text; a frame needs only 30% of its bright pixels to be text lines; and text sitting almost entirely in the bottom band, such as burned-in subtitles, is not credits. Moving credits are recognised more readily: the row profiles are smoothed over three rows so small, slowly moving text still matches, a third of the frames showing text is enough, and card changes as small as a thousandth of the picture count. Nothing is applied yet: the analysis still only reports, and the reader and re-analysis of stored results follow in phase 2b's next part.
