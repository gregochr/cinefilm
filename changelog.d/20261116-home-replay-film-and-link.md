### Added — The home replay's stand-in film and link

`scripts/replay/` now holds the first half of the home replay rig: a stand-in server that plays a film made of
padding at the pace a real journey's link managed, rebuilt minute by minute from the playback-health record of the
12 November train viewing. It is a measuring tool run by hand, not part of the app; the part that drives the real
player in a browser follows. `docs/engineering/home-replay.md` says what is real, what stands in and what is
approximate.
