### Added — Admin Playback shows what is playing now, with "Stop this stream"

The Playback section now opens with a "Now playing" card: each stream's film, device, where you are watching and the quality, with "Stop this stream" (and what it does) wherever the film is being converted on the fly. Direct play shows no Stop. The player also tells the server whether the device has a touch screen, so an iPad that identifies itself as a Mac is named "Safari on iPad" in Admin instead of "Safari on Mac".
