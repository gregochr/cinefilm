### Changed — collections move into Library

Collections no longer sit at the end of Home. Library now has two tabs, **Films** (`/library`) and **Collections** (`/library/collections`), each with a count; the Collections tab searches by name, sorts by name, recently added to or size, and shows all, complete or missing-films collections, with cards that fan up to three posters (earliest release in front, dimmed behind with a brightness filter, never an empty slot). The Collection filter on the Films tab is gone, replaced by a **Group collections** switch (off by default, remembered in the browser) that folds each collection into one tile, placed by title, year or date added, counting only the films that pass the filters and ignored while searching. A trailing " Collection" is dropped from TMDB's names everywhere. API: `GET /api/collections` is new, `/api/home` loses `collections`, and `/api/films` loses the `collection` parameter and `facets.collections` and gains `FilmCard.collectionId`.
