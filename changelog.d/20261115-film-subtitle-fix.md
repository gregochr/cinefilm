### Added — Fix a film's subtitles from its page, and report bad ones from the player

A quiet "Fix…" next to Subtitles on a film's page opens that film's subtitle files, with "Use my file instead", "Add my own file…", "Preview lines", "Delete this download" and "Let CineFilm decide". In the player, while a downloaded subtitle is showing, "Subtitles are wrong" in the subtitles menu deletes that download, stops CineFilm fetching the same file again and tells you in one line, without interrupting the film. Admin's "Show all subtitle files" keeps its tools for now.
