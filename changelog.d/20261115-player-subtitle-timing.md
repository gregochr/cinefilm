### Added — Move subtitles earlier or later while you watch

When a subtitle file is showing (your own or a download), the player's Audio and subtitles panel now has "Earlier", "Later" and "Reset timing", with the current timing beside them. Each press moves the lines a quarter of a second. The new timing loads beside the lines already showing and takes over once it has arrived, so the lines never vanish on a slow link; if it can't be loaded, the old timing stays and the panel says so. The film is not paused, sought or reloaded, and nothing but that one subtitle is fetched. The film's page data now carries each subtitle file's timing, so the panel needs no extra request. Changing a file's timing clears your "Looks right" on it (by design), so CineFilm may play a different subtitle file next time. Admin's own timing buttons stay for now.
