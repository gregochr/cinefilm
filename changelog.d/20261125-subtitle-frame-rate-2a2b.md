### Added — the other subtitle comparisons know how to compare a subtitle that has been retimed

The choice of a download to promote, the timing measurements against OpenSubtitles' files, and the fit of a subtitle
to OpenSubtitles' files now follow the same rule as the file check: a retimed subtitle is compared on the right
timeline, or not at all when the frame rates are not known well enough. Nothing is retimed yet, so nothing visible
changes: every comparison gives the same answer as before.
