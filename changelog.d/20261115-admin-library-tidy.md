### Changed — Admin's Library page is tidied into four cards

The Library scan card now says "Last scan 12 min ago · took 1m 48s", and its Protected, Needs conversion and Unreadable counts open a plain list of those films. "Scan library now" is the only amber button, and while a scan runs its progress takes the button's place. What the last scan changed (split, joined and flagged films) folds into a closed "What the last scan changed" section on that card, 25 at a time. Ratings, Import from Plex and a new Cast & crew card follow: Ratings says when IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes were last updated, Import from Plex shows whether Plex answers and what the last import brought across with its full report behind a "Last report" button ("Import credits from Plex", which copies where Plex says the end credits start, stays in that card), and Cast & crew says how many films have credits ("530 of 538 films have credits"). "What the last scan changed" now lists exactly what the last scan changed, not the latest twenty changes of any kind. The credits-detection training job is not offered in Admin.
