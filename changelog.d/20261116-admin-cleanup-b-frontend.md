### Changed — Admin cleanup, player and menus

The player's Audio, Subtitles and Quality menus now hold only their choices for screen readers: the headings, the
timing readout and the problem lines sit beside the menu, still announced. "Earlier" and "Later" turn off at the
10-minute limit instead of doing nothing. Fix match, Edit editions, the need rows and Admin's to do list show the
server's own words when the library is busy (for example "Subtitles are being changed or checked; try again in a
moment") instead of always blaming a scan. A subtitle report that cannot say which download it showed now says the
subtitle changed, and the dev mock words the Continue watching range error like the server.
