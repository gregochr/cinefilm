### Added — a full bank is refilled one rung higher

When the bank is full (about eight minutes) at a rung below what the link could carry, Auto now throws away the film from five minutes ahead of the playhead on and fetches it again one rung up, never above 480p, instead of waiting for it to play out. It needs three times the next rung's peak rate, no fragment loading and two quiet minutes. It stops at once and waits ten minutes if the bank falls under 270 s, a fragment fails, the player stalls or seeks, or the network changes. Manual quality never does this.
