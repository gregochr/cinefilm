### Added — "Leave unmatched" for films with no TMDB entry

Films that TMDB does not know (home videos, featurettes, concerts) no longer sit in Admin's "Need a match" list for ever. Each row has a quiet "Leave unmatched" button: the film stops being listed, stays in your library, is never retried by a scan or a Plex import, and can still be matched later from Fix match.
