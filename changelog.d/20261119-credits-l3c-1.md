### Added — credits classifier: CreditsSegmenter and the nested models

Adds `CreditsSegmenter`, the pure code that turns a film's per-frame credits probabilities into a start: the best stretch, leaning late, leading islands dropped, the confidence check and the dense search, with `SegmenterParams` for its six calibrated numbers. `CreditsTrainMain` now also writes `nested-models.json`, ten models each trained on the rows of the two folds left in, for the evaluation's nested selection. Nothing in the server calls either.
