### Changed — subtitle downloads follow the OpenSubtitles account's real daily allowance

CineFilm planned 90 OpenSubtitles downloads a day, but a free account allows 20, so OpenSubtitles stopped the
downloads before the shares between jobs ever applied, and the comparisons of existing files could use up the day
first. Now the daily limit is what the account's login says it allows (20 until that is known), and the shares are
taken from it: films whose own subtitle file was just dropped (they show no forced subtitles at all) are downloaded
first, then films with none, and comparing files uses at most 30% of the day. When OpenSubtitles reports fewer
downloads left than CineFilm counted, because the account is used elsewhere, CineFilm counts them too. A VIP account
would raise the limit by itself, with nothing to change; the old daily-limit setting now only caps it.
