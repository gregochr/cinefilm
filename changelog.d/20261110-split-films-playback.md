### Added — Films split across several files now play as one continuous film

A film that is stored as several files (the Extended Edition of a trilogy, "pt1" and "pt2") now plays through from the
first file into the next without a break: one scrubber, one runtime, one resume position and one set of subtitles for
the whole film, with no part numbers anywhere. Film detail reads "Extended Edition · 2 parts · 3h 55m · 1080p HEVC".
Parts are joined on their video, so the picture stays in step across the join, and each part's soundtrack is padded to
the end of its picture so the sound does too. Such a film always plays as an adaptive stream (it cannot be played as
the raw file), so Original is offered only when the parts match and can be copied. Existing files are probed once more
on the next library scan to record how long their video is.
