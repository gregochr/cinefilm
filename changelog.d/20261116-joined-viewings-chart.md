### Changed — the chart shows a viewing as one column, with its parts

A film watched in more than one playback (the player reloaded, or you paused and came back) is now one column in Admin's "How films played". The selected viewing shows each part's minute strip with its start and end times, and a quiet line between parts ("Picked up again after 6 s" or "Paused 1h 20m"). The player also reports where in the film each viewing started and ended, which is how a later playback is recognised as the same viewing.
