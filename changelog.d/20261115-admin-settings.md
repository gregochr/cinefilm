### Changed — Admin's Settings page follows the new design

Settings now shows "Continue watching starts after" with its short explanation and a minutes box (0 to 30, saved when you leave the box or press Enter, with the server's own message if a save is refused), a second card reading "Signed in on this browser" with a Sign out button and the note that signing out here doesn't affect other devices, and the TMDB credit at the bottom. The two switches in the old mock-up were never real settings and are left out.
