### Added — Admin Playback: "How films played" chart and the per-minute view (Admin redesign 5a-2)

Playback gains the chart of the film sessions of the last 30 or 90 days (All / Home / Away / Train): one column per session, oldest to newest, stacked by the time at Original, 1080p, 720p and 480p or lower in neutral tones, with a stall dot sized by the stall time and the environment letter under it. Tiles above count sessions, those without a stall, those that stalled (and where, when they all share a place), the typical start and the share of time at Original or 1080p. Choosing a column (hover, click or the arrow keys) shows that session below with a minute-by-minute strip of speed, quality played and stalls, read from its full record (rows of seven figures, or five in an older record). Each part loads on its own with its own error banner. The chart route's rows now carry the player's `touch` hint (left out when a report has none), so an iPad is named as one; the dev mock API serves the chart and records.
