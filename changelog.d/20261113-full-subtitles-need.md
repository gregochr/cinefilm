### Added — which films need full English subtitles

Admin now has a "Full subtitles" list beside the forced-subtitle one. It shows every film that was not made in English,
or that you have chosen for, and says whether it needs full English subtitles. "Needed" means the audio of your copy is
in another language, "Not needed" means the audio is English (a dub, say), and "Unsure" means the file does not say
what language the audio is in. A film with two editions counts as Needed if either edition's audio is foreign. Each row
gives the reason in plain words and the audio language of each edition, and you can tap Needed or Not needed to
overrule it (tap again to go back to automatic). The film's page data carries the answer too, ready for the player.
Nothing plays differently yet, and the forced-subtitle rules are unchanged.
