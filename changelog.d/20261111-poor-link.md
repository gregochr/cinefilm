### Changed — a poor connection retries instead of giving up on streaming

On a weak link the player now keeps trying under "Reconnecting…" instead of falling back to direct play. A segment
that stops moving for 12 seconds is given up on and retried. The measured speed is remembered for ten minutes, so the
next film skips the probe; with nothing measured, a film starts on the lowest quality and steps up. The slow-connection
card opens only for a speed that was actually measured. The server now compresses text responses (gzip).
