### Added — Mark a film as having no English subtitles

A foreign-language film that has no English subtitles anywhere can now be marked as such: the new "No English
subtitles exist" button (on the Subtitles foreign-language tab and on the To do "English subtitles missing" rows) marks it
done and stops CineFilm searching for them. You can still search OpenSubtitles or add your own file for it, and "Let
CineFilm decide" under "Your choices" undoes it. The To do rows for missing English subtitles now offer the same actions
as the Subtitles tab: search, "Add my own file…", "It's English" and the new button.
