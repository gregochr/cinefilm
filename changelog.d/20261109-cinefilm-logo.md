### Added — CineFilm logo, tab icons and the Gate buffering animation

CineFilm now has a logo (the Gate): tab and home-screen icons with a web-app manifest, the mark beside the wordmark in the header, and on the sign-in, empty-library, error and not-found screens. The player's buffering spinner is replaced by the Gate animation (a lamp, flickering beam and advancing film strip), shown only after 400 ms of continuous buffering and still under reduced motion.
