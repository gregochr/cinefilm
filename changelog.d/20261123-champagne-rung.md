### Changed — Auto climbs above 480p only on headroom (720p is the champagne rung)

The player no longer steps up to 720p or above on a deep bank alone. A rung above 480p needs the link's recent fragment rate (from the first byte, retried fragments left out) at 3 times the rung's average bitrate, and the bank nearly full for the rung below; it keeps the rung at 2 times, and drops to the highest rung that still passes when the link sags. On the 23 Nov train run Auto climbed to 720p six times at 4 Mbit/s, which idled the link while the bank fell to 256 s and ended in 240p spells. Now the bank stays near eight minutes at 480p on such a link, and a fast home link still climbs to the top, a rung a minute. The start rung is held at 480p until a fragment has proved the link. No stall first, quality second.
