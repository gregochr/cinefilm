### Changed — The player loads the light build of hls.js

The first play parses less code: the hls.js chunk goes from 578,153 bytes (177,730 gzipped) to 361,950 (113,740
gzipped), 63,990 bytes smaller over the wire. The main `index` chunk is unchanged (434,479 to 434,485 bytes; 133,945
to 133,948 gzipped). The light build drops what CineFilm never sends: alternate audio and subtitle renditions, EME,
CMCD, interstitials, I-frame playlists, variable substitution, Media Capabilities, and HEVC and AC-3 in MPEG-TS. A
copy of HEVC video, AC-3 passthrough in TS segments or `#EXT-X-MEDIA` renditions would need the full build again.
