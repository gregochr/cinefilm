### Added — Leave out, put back and remove Christmas films, with Undo

On the Admin "Christmas films" screen each film has one button: Leave out (a film TMDB tags), Remove (one you added) or Put back (one you left out). Every change shows a toast with Undo that stays for 8 seconds (longer while the pointer or keyboard is on it), a refused change (for example while a scan runs) says why and changes nothing, and focus goes back to the film's button after an Undo.
