### Added — credits classifier: logistic regression for model B (L4b-4b)

`LogisticRegression` and `LrParams` (package `credits.learn`) are the head model B will train on image embeddings: L2-penalised logistic regression (the bias is not penalised) fitted by L-BFGS with memory 10 and a backtracking line search. Rows stay `float` and are standardised on the fly, never copied; the objective and its gradient come from one pass over the rows. A fit stops on the gradient, the iteration limit or the line search and records why. The model reads and writes JSON (`dimension`, `base`, `params`, `mean`, `scale`, `weights`, `bias`, `iterations`, `stopped`), and `base()` means what the tree model's `base()` means. Nothing calls it yet (`CreditsTrainLrMain` is L4b-5). Tests pin the standardisation, the objective and gradient on a hand-computed case, the gradient against finite differences, the optimum against Newton's method, each stop reason, each refusal and the JSON round trip.
