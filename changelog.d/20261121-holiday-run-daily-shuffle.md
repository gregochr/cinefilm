### Fixed — the holiday theme runs to the day, and Christmas changes daily

Halloween now runs from the Monday on or before 24 Oct through 31 Oct, and Christmas from the Monday on or before
18 Dec through 25 Dec, instead of ending on the Sunday (Home showed the ordinary theme from 21 to 25 Dec). The
Christmas list now shows the films you added by hand first, then a different mix each day (a shuffle seeded by the
London date), the same on Home and on the Christmas films screen.
