### Added — Full English subtitles are downloaded for foreign films

A film that needs full English subtitles and has none beside it now gets them from OpenSubtitles. They are searched
for and downloaded in the same daily run as the forced ones, ahead of them in the day's download budget, and they play
straight away. Checking them comes next: for now each download is only measured against a second OpenSubtitles file and
the result is written to the log, so the owner can see how well they line up before anything acts on it. A subtitle the
owner puts beside the film always wins over a download, and removing a download in Admin makes CineFilm try the next
file.
