### Added — Halloween and Christmas weeks on Home

In Halloween week (the Monday on or before 24 October, to the Sunday) Home's "This week" row is horror films, and in Christmas week (the Monday on or before 18 December) it is Christmas films: those TMDB tags `christmas`, plus or minus the owner's overrides (exclude beats include beats the keyword). Fewer than five matching films and the normal weekly rotation shows instead. The theme comes back with `kind` `holiday` and `value` `halloween` or `christmas`.
