### Changed — a collection needs two films in the library

TMDB puts any film that has a sequel into a collection (A Dog's Purpose, for one), so a single film used to produce a one-film "collection" on Home, in the Library filter and a link on its detail page. A collection now counts only when at least two of its films are in the library: below that it gets no Home card, no Library filter entry and no collection link on Film detail. The collection page itself still answers, so old links keep working.
