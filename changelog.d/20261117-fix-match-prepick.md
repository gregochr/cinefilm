### Added — Fix match picks the film that matches your file's length

Fix match now shows how long your file is beside its name (for example "File: Enchanted.m4v · 1h 47m") and pre-picks
a result for you. If exactly one result runs about as long as your file (within eight minutes), that one is picked and
marked "Same length as your file". Otherwise CineFilm's own best guess is picked and marked "CineFilm's best guess",
unless its length clearly does not fit the file; if neither applies, nothing is picked. Nothing is applied until you
press "Use this match", and you can pick any other result first.
