### Changed — subtitles decide whether a film needs them by themselves

You no longer need to decide by hand which films need subtitles. Forced subtitles now follow what exists online: a film
OpenSubtitles has been asked about and has no forced files for does not need them, and neither does a film that was not
made in English, because its full English subtitles already carry those lines. A real forced file or track still wins.
Films not yet checked online show as "Checking online" until the daily search reaches them. Full English subtitles are
now needed whenever a foreign-language film's audio is not labelled English, since your copies keep the original audio:
if one turns out to be a dub, switch it off once. Your own choice still overrides all of this, and is now optional.
