### Added — Christmas films screen in Admin (read only)

Admin has a "Christmas films" section (between Library and Playback, `/admin/christmas`) that shows which films Home's Christmas week will show: how many, the 12-place meter, and how far TMDB keyword fetching has got. Three lists (In Christmas week, Added by me, Left out by me) show each film with the reason it counts, and films past the 12th are marked as not fitting. Leaving a film out and putting it back follow in a later change.
