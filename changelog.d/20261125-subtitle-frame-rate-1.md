### Added — each downloaded subtitle keeps the frame rate it was timed for

CineFilm now remembers the frame rate OpenSubtitles states for every subtitle it downloads, and fills it in for the
ones already downloaded where the search results still hold it. It also keeps a note of downloads you timed by hand,
so a later change leaves them alone. This is the first step towards subtitles that follow a 25 fps copy of a film;
nothing changes in playback yet.
