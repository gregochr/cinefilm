### Added — https://cinefilm.chrisjgregory.dev, tailnet only

CineFilm is now served at `https://cinefilm.chrisjgregory.dev` by a new `cinefilm-proxy` compose service: Caddy (2.11.8, built in `deploy/caddy/` with the pinned `caddy-dns/cloudflare` v0.2.4 module, pushed to GHCR as `cinefilm-caddy:<release tag>` by `deploy.yml` and build-checked on every PR). It runs on the host network, binds only the two tailnet addresses on 443 (never 0.0.0.0, the LAN or port 80), gets its certificate by Cloudflare DNS challenge, streams video untouched and keeps cookies and Authorization out of its logs. It receives only the token, domain and tailnet addresses, not the whole `.env`. `deploy.sh` now needs `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN`, `CINEFILM_TAILNET_IPV4` and `CINEFILM_TAILNET_IPV6` in `~/cinefilm/.env` (exit 4 before any change if missing), pulls both images, and the post-deploy smoke also probes the app through the domain (proxy-only failures exit 3 and never roll back). `tailscale serve` moves to :8443 as the fallback. `operations.md` has the owner's one-time "Custom domain" steps, verification and rollback; `SECURITY.md` rule 6 is updated; `scripts/test/caddy-config.test.sh` pins the safety properties.

The proxy image starts through `entrypoint.sh`, which refuses a malformed `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` (not 40 to 200 URL-safe characters) with a value-free error before Caddy can echo it into `docker logs`; `deploy.sh` applies the same rule and exits 4.
