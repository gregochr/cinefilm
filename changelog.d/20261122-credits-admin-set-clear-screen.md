### Added — set and clear an end-credits time from Admin

Opening a row in the End credits list now has a time field, filled in with the marker or the default end-screen point: "Show stills" reloads both frames at the time you type, and "Set this time" saves it as your own. A time that is not `h:mm:ss` or `m:ss`, or that falls outside where credits can start, says so and sends nothing. On a row with your own time, "Clear my time" goes back to Plex's marker, or to none. After either, a short message confirms it, the list and counts read again and focus lands on the next row. The Library card follows.
