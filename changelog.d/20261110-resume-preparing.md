### Fixed — Details offers Resume on a film you are re-watching, and the player shows where it will resume

A film you had already watched, and are now watching again, keeps its saved position, so the Details page now offers "Resume from …" (and "Start from beginning") instead of only "Play". The player shows the resume point on its clock, remaining time and scrubber from the moment it opens, instead of 00:00:00. While a film is starting the indicator reads "PREPARING" rather than "BUFFERING", which is kept for a stall after playback has begun. Leaving the player with Back now waits briefly (up to 1.5 seconds) for the final position to be saved, so the Details page no longer shows a stale position. Marking a film watched clears its resume point straight away. Progress saves are now sent one at a time, in order, so an older position can never overwrite a newer one.
