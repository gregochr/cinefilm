### Changed — Admin's Subtitles page lists only what CineFilm couldn't settle

Subtitles now has two tabs, "Foreign-language films" and "Forced subtitles not found", each listing only the films CineFilm flagged as needing you, with a count on the tab. A foreign-language film offers "Add my own file…" (pick one of your subtitle files for that film) and, when its audio isn't labelled, "It's English"; a forced-subtitle film offers "No foreign scenes". Either choice removes the row and stays removed. The filter chips are gone. "Everything else" holds the counts (foreign films that have English subtitles, films in English, films being checked online now), a read-only list of those films, the OpenSubtitles search status (moved from the Library scan card), and "Show all subtitle files", which keeps every per-file tool and lists 50 files at a time with "Show more".
