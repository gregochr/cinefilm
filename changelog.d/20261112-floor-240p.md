### Added — a 240p floor for every film

The first train journey in the playback-health record showed the link falling to about 0.3 Mbit/s for minutes at a
time, under half of what the lowest quality needed, and the film stalling through each of those spells. Every film
above 240 lines now has a 240p quality at about a third of a megabit (250 kbit/s of picture, 64 of sound), a little
under half of 360p. Auto drops to it when the link cannot carry more, and the Quality menu lists it. A film of 240
lines or fewer is unchanged.

It is the first of four steps (`docs/engineering/train-link-plan.md`). Not checked on the server's encoder yet: how
the picture looks at that rate, and the encoder's speed with one more quality to make.
