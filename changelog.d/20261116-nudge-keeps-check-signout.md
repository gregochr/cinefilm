### Changed — a timing nudge keeps your "Looks right" check; signing out on an expired session shows the sign-in card

Nudging a subtitle's timing (Earlier, Later or Reset) no longer takes away your "Looks right" check, so a proven forced
file stays proven and keeps playing. Attaching a file to another film, edition or part, detaching it, or a change to the
file on disk still clears the check. A 401 on sign-out, from anything in front of the server, is now treated as signed
out and shows the sign-in card (the server itself answers 200 for an expired session); other failures still say
"Couldn't sign out".
