### Added — a retime job for downloaded subtitles, switched off

A new job, set by `cinefilm.subtitles.retime`, can put a downloaded subtitle on the film's own timeline (a 23.976 fps
subtitle on a 25 fps copy, and the reverse), keep the original so it can be put back, and leave alone anything the
owner timed by hand. The setting is `off`, so nothing is rewritten and nothing visible changes yet.
