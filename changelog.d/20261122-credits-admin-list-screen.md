### Added — an End credits list in Admin

Admin has a new End credits list, at `/admin/library/credits` for now (its Library card follows), of every film edition and its end-credits marker: where it came from, the time, or why there is none. Tabs filter the list and it pages 25 at a time. "Look and set…" opens a row to show two frames of the film, at the marker's time and 30 seconds before it, so you can tell the credits from the film. Setting and clearing a time from this screen comes in the next release.
