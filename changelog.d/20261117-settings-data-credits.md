### Added — Settings credits OpenSubtitles, OMDb and IPinfo

Admin → Settings now credits the other outside services CineFilm uses, below the TMDB line: OpenSubtitles for subtitles,
OMDb for IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings (licensed CC BY-NC 4.0), and IPinfo for the player's network lookup. Each name
links to its site and opens in a new tab.
